Cheshire, Connecticut

Welcome to Cheshire

Cheshire is a town of about 28,700 people in New Haven County, Connecticut — a community settled in 1694, incorporated in 1780, and still built around the same idea it started with: neighbors looking after the place they share.

Whether you live here, are thinking about moving here, or are just passing through, you'll find the essentials in one place — what's happening, where to go, who to call, and which local businesses are worth knowing.

About Cheshire

A town with deep roots

Cheshire began as New Cheshire Parish, part of Wallingford, and became its own town in May 1780. Its name comes from Cheshire, England. More than three centuries later, it covers about 33.4 square miles of land and water, and it is governed by a council-manager form of government.

A nine-member Town Council works with Town Manager Sean M. Kimball.
