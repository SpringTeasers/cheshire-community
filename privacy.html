How this site handles information

Privacy Policy

This is a simple, static community directory. Here is what it does and does not do with information.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What this site is

The Cheshire, CT Community Directory is a static, community-maintained informational website. It publishes a friendly guide to Cheshire, Connecticut — local businesses, parks and trails, town services, and events. It is not a government website and it has no user accounts, logins, or member features.

Information we collect

None. This site does not set cookies, does not run analytics, and does not include third-party trackers, advertising scripts, or social media pixels. No personal information is collected through the site and nothing you type into it is stored.

The contact form

The contact form on this site is a non-functional static placeholder. It is included to show the finished page layout only — it does not submit anything, and any message entered into it is not sent, saved, or delivered to anyone. To reach the town, use the Cheshire Town Hall details below.

Links to other sites

This directory links to third-party websites, such as local businesses and official town resources. Those sites are not operated by us and have their own privacy policies. Once you follow a link away from this site, this policy no longer applies.
