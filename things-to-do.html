Seasonal highlights

The Cheshire Fall Festival brings the town together each harvest season, and the Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony opens the holidays. Both are annual, and exact dates vary year to year.

Mark the calendar

Seasonal events

Fall

Cheshire Fall Festival

A harvest-season celebration with local vendors and activities for the whole family. Held annually — exact dates vary by year, so check town announcements closer to the season.

Winter

Christmas Tree Lighting Ceremony

A town gathering that marks the start of the holiday season, with the community turning out together. Scheduled yearly — dates vary, so confirm with the town before you go.

Before you go

Event dates and park hours can change. For the most current information, check the official town website or the town's own announcements.
