What's confirmed

  • September 30, 2026 · posted September 9, 2026

    Multi-Modal Transportation Plan — Public Meeting

    The town is holding a public meeting on its Multi-Modal Transportation Plan — an opportunity for residents to hear about and comment on how people move around Cheshire by car, on foot, by bike, and by transit.
